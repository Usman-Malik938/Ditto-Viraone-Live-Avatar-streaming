## Company

### About Smart4U
> What does Smart4U do? What kind of company are you? What services do you offer?
Smart4U is an internet marketing and modern technology agency. We help businesses grow online by creating websites, running advertising campaigns, producing promotional and AI videos, developing SCARD digital business profiles, and developing the ViraOne solution.

### Experience
> How long have you been in business? How many websites have you made? Who are your clients?
We have more than 10 years of experience and have delivered more than 1,100 websites. We have done hundreds of video projects, including projects using AI, and we have hundreds of active hosting and business email customers. For selected clients we provide comprehensive service: website, advertising, video, SCARD and other services. Our growth comes mainly from recommendations and long-term relationships. We also build custom web applications, for example a system for the gardening industry to manage tasks and work schedules.

## Websites

### WordPress websites
> Do you build websites? What platform do you use? Can I edit the website myself? Is it mobile friendly?
We build websites exclusively on WordPress, mainly using Elementor: one-page sites, business websites, multi-page sites, landing pages and larger websites. Additional modules such as forms, calendars or booking systems may be possible by individual arrangement. Our websites are adapted for mobile devices. Customers can edit content themselves in the WordPress CMS or ask Smart4U to do it.

### What the website price depends on
> How much does a website cost? What affects the price? Why does a website cost more? I need a website with booking.
The website price depends on the requirements, the number of subpages and additional functionality such as calculators, booking or other custom features. Useful questions to understand the need: is it a new website or a redesign, what industry, how many subpages, which features, and does the customer already have a domain, hosting, texts and photos.

### Website care after launch: hosting, domain, updates and content changes
> How much does it cost to maintain a website? Do you offer hosting? Who updates the website? Do you make backups? What does a website cost per year?
After launch, a website needs hosting and a domain. The hosting plans and their details are at jethost.pl. Optional technical updates keep WordPress and its plugins up to date and include backups; they matter most for websites that run paid ads and for busy online stores. Content changes and other small jobs are billed by the hour. The customer can also edit content alone in WordPress.

### Legal package
> Do you do GDPR? Privacy policy for my website? Cookie banner? What is the legal package?
The legal package includes updates to legal documents. It covers GDPR, privacy policies, cookies, clauses and consent management mechanisms (CMP), as appropriate to the service scope. Buying the package does not automatically make the whole company fully legally compliant. For the most serious GDPR violations, regulations allow a maximum fine of up to EUR 20 million or 4% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher, but not every irregularity leads to such a fine.

### Online stores
> Do you build online stores? Can you make an e-commerce shop? WooCommerce?
We have experience delivering online stores. We assess the scope and feasibility individually. The platform, timeline and price of an online store are confirmed by the team after assessing the scope.

## Advertising

### Google Ads campaigns focused on customer enquiries
> Do you run Google Ads? Can you get me more customers from Google? How does Google advertising work? Who pays for the clicks?
We adapt Google Ads campaigns to the customer's business and focus primarily on generating measurable customer enquiries: phone calls, contact form submissions, enquiries, reservations and other relevant conversions. We set up conversion tracking and optimize campaigns based on it. Our team has 8 years of experience with Google Ads. We run Google Search ads in search results, Performance Max (PMax) campaigns and campaigns in Poland and international markets. The advertising budget is paid directly to Google and the customer can see the spending; our management fee is separate.

### SEO and Google Business Profile
> Do you do SEO? Can you get my website higher on Google? Google Maps listing? Google Business Profile?
Website SEO and Google Business Profile optimization are delivered together with a trusted team of specialists.

### Meta Ads: Facebook and Instagram advertising
> Do you do Facebook ads? Instagram advertising? Can you promote my business on Facebook?
We run advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram (Meta Ads). This includes campaign setup, management and optimization, as well as advertising graphics and video materials. The advertising budget is paid directly to Meta; our management fee is separate. Meta Ads advertising is different from social media profile management: we do not generally provide ongoing organic profile management, daily posting or community management as a standard service.

### Advertising in ChatGPT
> Can you advertise my business in ChatGPT? AI search advertising?
Advertising in ChatGPT is a new service area. The team confirms availability and launch conditions each time.

### Advertising materials
> I don't have photos for an ad. Who makes the ad graphics? Can you use my existing video in ads?
As part of the advertising service we prepare standard copy, graphics, ad layouts and materials using stock photos, so the customer does not need their own photos. The customer answers a few questions and the team organizes the rest. The customer's existing videos can be used in a campaign without a separate production fee. New advertising videos, reels and AI videos are an additional paid service.

### Guarantees of advertising results
> Do you guarantee results? How many leads will I get? Will I be first on Google?
We focus on measurable results, but we do not guarantee specific search rankings, numbers of leads, sales or advertising returns.

## Video

### Video production and AI videos
> Do you make videos? Can you make a video from photos? Reels for Instagram? YouTube video? Drone filming? Can you come to my city to film?
We produce traditional videos from footage (camera, gimbal, drone), advertising reels, animations and AI-generated videos. ## SCARD

### What SCARD is
> What is SCARD? Digital business card? NFC card? Do I need an app? Does SCARD replace a website?
SCARD is a digital business profile, not just a traditional business card. It presents services, contact details, photos and videos, social media, location, links and an option to save the contact. It can be shared by link, QR code and NFC, and no app installation is required. SCARD can complement an existing website; it does not replace it.

### What SCARD includes and what the customer provides
> What is included in SCARD? Who updates my SCARD? I don't have time to create a SCARD. Do you have metal cards?
The yearly SCARD subscription includes preparing the profile, maintenance and 24/7 availability, updates to details, offers, photos and videos by the Smart4U team, and an NFC sticker. Metal cards are not part of the standard subscription. The customer does not need to design or configure anything: they provide basic company details, services and offer information, send photos if they have them, and if not, Smart4U can prepare the materials.

Video: we make promotional and advertising videos and reels, and a shoot in another city is arranged individually.
